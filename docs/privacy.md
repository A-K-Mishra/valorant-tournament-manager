# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

This policy explains how **aBlitz's Challenges** handles information when you participate in community events or interact with its Discord bot.

## 1. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, the Service may process:
- Discord user ID, username and server membership information.
- VALORANT/Riot ID supplied by you for event administration.
- Registration details, team assignments and event participation.
- Challenge claims, match results and evidence files or links you submit.
- Moderator decisions, timestamps and basic bot interaction logs.

Please do not submit passwords, authentication codes, government identity documents, financial account credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## 2. Why information is used

Information is used to:
- Register participants and administer events.
- Review claims, evidence and results.
- Maintain leaderboards, brackets and event histories.
- Prevent duplicate, fraudulent or abusive submissions.
- Respond to moderation requests and troubleshoot the Service.

## 3. Evidence and visibility

Evidence may be visible to event administrators and moderators for review. Approved results, leaderboard entries, team names or player identifiers may be published in the Discord community. Event announcements should explain what information will be made public.

Avoid including unrelated personal information in screenshots or recordings.

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell participant personal information. Information may be visible to Discord and its infrastructure providers as part of using Discord, and may be shared with authorised moderators when needed to administer the community. We may disclose information where required by law or to address security, fraud or safety concerns.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Event records may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to maintain competition results, resolve disputes, prevent repeated abuse and keep an accurate audit history. Evidence and other records should be deleted when no longer needed, subject to legitimate record-keeping needs and applicable law.

To request access, correction or deletion of information held by the organisers, contact the moderators through the official Discord server. Some records may need to be retained where there is a legitimate reason or legal obligation.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable measures to limit access to event data to authorised organisers. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to bot credentials must be restricted, and secrets must never be stored in public source control.

## 7. Children

The Service is not intended to be used in violation of Discord's age requirements or applicable child-protection laws. Do not register if you are not eligible to use Discord in your location.

## 8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the bot and community features evolve. The latest version will be published at this location.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the moderators through the official aBlitz's Challenges Discord server.
